# Credits for the animated unit sprites (LPC)

The unit sprite sheets in this folder (`*_blue_*.png`, `*_red_*.png`) were assembled by
`tools/make_units.gd` from parts of the Liberated Pixel Cup (LPC) collection, via the
Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator
(https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator).
Some parts were recoloured (team tints). The assembled sheets are distributed under
**CC-BY-SA 3.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/); every part's own
licence options and authors are listed below, as the LPC licences require.
