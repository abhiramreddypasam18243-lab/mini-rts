# Assets and Third-Party Code (SPEC section 3, L1 to L3)

Every file in `assets/` and every piece of borrowed code or tooling is listed here with its source and licence.
The game world, HUD and effects use three CC0 art packs by Kenney (decisions D-38 and D-39). The units are animated Liberated Pixel Cup (LPC) characters (decision D-41): they are **CC-BY-SA 3.0**, not CC0, so every artist is credited in `assets/lpc/CREDITS.md` and in the game's Credits screen. Bars, rings and highlights are drawn in code.
